## Mechanism checks and limits of their interpretation

Variability: clean within-entity mean is preserved; sample SD, MAD, IQR and range scale by e, variance by e^2; CV scales by e when its mean denominator remains valid. Median becomes m_i+e(median_i-m_i). Thus normalized MAD/IQR generally do NOT scale by e: their denominators change. Check the exact transformed-median denominator formula instead. Do not assert geometric-mean invariance.

Heterogeneity: each entity mean becomes c+e(m_i-c); within-entity residuals, SD, MAD, IQR and range are preserved. Across affected-group arithmetic entity means, the sample variance scales by e^2; its group mean shifts by (e-1)*(original_group_mean-c). With the fixed equal group counts, the original group-mean difference Delta becomes ((1+e)/2)*Delta=1.075*Delta. Record the original and changed difference and this prediction. This incidental finite-half mean shift can influence rank ordering. Relative spread metrics can change under the resulting entity-specific location shifts and do not measure pure between-entity variance. Median/trimmed mean shift by the entity-specific additive shift; geometric mean is not generally translation-equivariant.

Location: additive translation preserves within-entity absolute spread and shifts arithmetic mean, median and trimmed mean by h(e-1). Relative spread and geometric mean need their actual formulas.

Mann–Whitney targets probabilistic ordering through U, not a dedicated variance/shape contrast. For independent distributions symmetric around the same center, unequal scale can retain ordering probability .5. The exact toy demonstrates a blind spot of that statistic for a specific deterministic configuration. It does not assert zero power, nominal finite-sample rejection rates, or no response to asymmetric retained pools. Report transformed mean-spread geometry beside U, so unchanged U cannot be misreported as a failed transform. This distinction is consistent with Fay and Proschan(2010), Statistics Surveys4:1–39, DOI10.1214/09-SS051, sections5.1.1–5.1.2 and AppendixD.2 (primary reference: https://healy.econ.ohio-state.edu/fayproschan.pdf). Ordinary WMW is not generally a valid test of ordering probability.5 under arbitrary unequal distributions; do not claim nominal or zero rejection probability for the scale alternative.
